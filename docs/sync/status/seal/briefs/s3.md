## Build

Fill every `unimplemented!()` in `membership.rs` and `receipt.rs`:

1. The canonical membership bytes (§13.2) and their strict parser: wrong magic or version, a list out
   of order or with duplicates, a flag other than 0 or 1, a count over its limit of §23, trailing
   bytes are `seal/membership_format`; every count is checked against the bytes remaining before
   anything is allocated. Equal states give equal bytes. The accessors of §26.
2. `classify` (§14): the seventeen steps of §14.2 in the table's order, each failure with exactly the
   class and code of its row; the bookkeeping of §13.5 for each class and nothing else; the flags
   `own_dev_foreign` (§14.7) and `based_on_unchecked` (§14.8); `Receipt.chain = C_s` for every class;
   the local error `seal/missing_root` as `Err`.
3. Genesis at seq 1 from the anchor (§13.3): the checks of SEAL-MEM-004, SEAL-MEM-005 and
   SEAL-KEY-017, and exactly the state §13.3 lists.
4. Every transition of §13.6 on a working copy, all or nothing, refused with the code of the first
   item that fails; author classes from `M_{s-1}` (§13.4); the limits (`seal/ctl_cap`,
   `seal/ctl_device_cap`); `device_no` (§13.7); epochs and stale-epoch entries (§13.8, §13.9); beats,
   active members and both modes of `RulesUpgrade` (§13.10); the recovery-key change with its 72-hour
   window from the log clock (§13.11); the recovery identity's powers and limits (§13.12); `ResetTo`
   and `Skip` recording only their targets (§13.13).
5. Skip targets (§14.6): the two passes, `NeedFrames` with every missing `(t, frame_hash)` and no
   change of state, the hash and author checks of a supplied frame.
6. Halts (§14.5): the commitment halt when this device is a recipient and its wrap does not open or
   the root does not match `commit`; the rules halt when the activated version is not in
   `supported_rules`. Both come with `Accept`. `learned_root` carries the root an accepted rotation's
   own wrap gave.

Lead rulings:

- Classification is a function of its inputs alone (SEAL-RCPT-006): no global state, no hash-map
  iteration order, no entropy.
- A frame can never make `classify` panic or return `Err` other than `seal/missing_root`; say in your
  report what `classify` does when its own caller breaks the contract (seq 0, no anchor at seq 1), and
  with which code.
- `classify` works on a copy and returns `M_s`; it never mutates its input.

## Tests and vectors

- One test per row of §14.2 (passing and failing), per row and per precondition of §13.6 (each
  refusal code once at least), per rule of §13.9 and §13.10, per path of §13.11, per power and limit
  of §13.12, and for the races of V2-8: two rotations for one epoch (the first in log order wins, the
  second is refused everywhere, its author's device included); a `DeviceAdded` sealed before a
  rotation and sequenced after it (refused as a stale-epoch control entry); a stale-epoch `cmd` inside
  and outside the 30 days.
- Determinism: two receivers with equal inputs give equal classes, codes, flags and membership bytes.
- Strict Ed25519 through `classify` (SEAL-GEN-010): a frame whose signature has a non-canonical `S`
  (the valid `S` plus the group order) is `Skip` with `seal/bad_signature`, and so is one whose `R` is
  a small-order point.
- `tamper`: flipping any byte of a frame never yields `Accept`, and never changes the membership
  beyond what §13.5 allows for the class returned. `parsers`: `Membership::from_canonical_bytes` and
  `classify` over random and mutated frames and membership bytes never panic.
- Vectors: `seal-membership.json`, `seal-receipt.json`, `seal-commit.json`, `seal-rotation.json`
  (§24's table says what each holds; for `seal-rotation.json` mind the draw order of §7: the root,
  then the 32 bytes of each wrap in ascending recipient-id order, then the frame's nonce). Add to
  `seal-padding.json`'s check, if task S2 left it for you, that each malformed plaintext sealed into a
  frame is refused with `seal/bad_plaintext`.
