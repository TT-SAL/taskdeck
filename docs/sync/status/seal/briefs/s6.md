## Tests and vectors

- `pairing_mitm` (a gate target, no cargo feature): a relay in the middle that knows the code and
  holds every slot. It substitutes its own keys towards E, towards N or both, re-computing the MACs.
  Show that E's and N's digits then differ unless the attacker guessed them (commit, then reveal: it
  must fix its keys before it sees either side's), that N aborts after the third wrong entry, and that
  the attack goes through exactly when the digits typed into N are N's own, so six digits are the
  whole barrier; also that E never posts slot C before slot B verified, and never slot D before the
  user confirmed.
- Unit and integration tests: both machines through a whole pairing against a simulated relay and
  board; every abort of §17.11; every check of §17.6 and §17.9 failing once in order; a rotation
  racing the pairing in both orders (`seal/pair_raced`); a slot replayed under another label or
  reflected to its sender.
- `tamper`: flipping any byte of any slot, the bundle included, aborts. `contexts`: the bundle does
  not open as an entry, a blob or a file record (SEAL-CTX-003). `parsers`: `parse_code` and both
  machines over random events and mutated slots never panic.
- Vectors: `seal-pairing.json`, `seal-code.json` (§24's table).
